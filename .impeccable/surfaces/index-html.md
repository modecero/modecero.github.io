# Homepage

**Mode:** Persuade. **Scope:** the site's single homepage (`index.html`) plus placeholder legal pages linked from its footer.

## Surface strategy

- **Audience:** publishers and partners triaging studios at a desk, short on time.
- **Job:** understand at a glance that Mode Cero is an independent studio from Spain making original games, see Whirlpop played, grab the press kit, email the studio.
- **Only call to action:** Email the studio (`mailto:hello@modecero.com`), in the header. No navigation menu, no Works or Studio sections: the statement is the studio description and the games list sits directly on the page.
- **Structure:**
  1. Header: logo and Email the studio.
  2. Statement and one supporting line.
  3. Games list: 01 Whirlpop, open, with the YouTube video in the CRT frame, the pitch, the facts and the press kit.
  4. Footer: socials, Legal notice, Privacy, © Mode Cero.
- **Proof:** the YouTube gameplay video (click-to-load, youtube-nocookie). The playable build is invitation-only and is never linked.
- **Ranges:** designed for 1 to about 12 games. New games become rows; unannounced games never appear.
- **Open, rendered as visible placeholders until provided:**
  - the YouTube URL
  - the press kit file (`press/whirlpop-presskit.zip`)
  - screenshots and a poster frame
  - social links
  - the legal texts

## Direction contract

THESIS: Mode Cero's own TV screen becomes the frame for every game it makes, on a quiet light page whose only raised voice is Email the studio. It refuses the category default: the dark neon trailer hero with a nav bar and a screenshot grid.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Palette:** cool neutral ground #f2f2f0 and ink #16131f, the logo's screen black.
- **The frame:** every game sits in the logo's CRT shape. That is a rounded rectangle with soft TV corners, filled with a dark radial screen with a highlight at the upper left and faint scanlines.
- **Glitch:** magenta #ff2bd6 and cyan #22d3ee exist only as RGB-split glitch slices, in motion and active states.
- **Type:** BBH Bogle in uppercase for display (the logo's face), Barlow for text.
- **Lines and controls:** 3px ink rules between games, and ink-filled buttons with 12px radii.

STORY: the visitor understands that this is an independent Spanish studio with original games, believes the work is real because they watch Whirlpop being played, and then emails hello@modecero.com with the press kit in hand.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- **Header:** the logo badge at the top left (about 76px tall), and Email the studio as an ink button at the top right.
- **Statement:** "Original games, made in Spain." in BBH Bogle at display size across about 60% of the width, with one supporting line beneath.
- **The fold:** a 3px rule opens the games list, so the 01 WHIRLPOP title and the top of its CRT screen sit at the fold, inviting the scroll.
- **Signature interaction:** pressing play on the CRT turns the TV on, a horizontal line opening to the full screen with one RGB slice, and then loads the video.
