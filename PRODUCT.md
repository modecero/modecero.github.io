## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS (user's choice). Hosted on GitHub Pages from the `modecero/modecero.github.io` repository, so there is no build step and no server-side code.

## Users

- **Primary: publishers and business partners.** People evaluating modecero for a publishing deal, funding, porting or another partnership. They come to judge the finished game and the studio behind it, and then decide whether to make contact.
- Players, press, content creators and potential hires were not chosen as priority audiences. The site may still serve them, but it is not designed around them.

## Product Purpose

modecero is a solo-developer video game studio. Its website presents the studio's work so that publishers and partners can assess it and get in touch. Success means a qualified partner understands the game, sees it in action, has what they need to evaluate it, and reaches out.

## Positioning

Mode Cero is an independent studio from Spain that makes original casual games. Anything beyond that (approach, track record) has not been stated yet; do not invent it.

It runs instantly on any device, mobile or desktop, and is built for the endless 'one more go.'"

  - **Playable build is invitation-only.** It must never be embedded or linked publicly on the site; the public site shows the game through its YouTube gameplay video only.
  - **Not provided yet:** art style, store or portal availability.

- There is no store presence yet (no Steam, itch.io, console or mobile pages). Contact and follow-up go through email and social links.
- Partners typically evaluate the game through gameplay footage and a press kit.

## Product Principles

1. **The studio is the subject.** modecero leads; each game is a work by the studio, never the site's whole identity. When showing a game, let partners see it played (footage over claims).
2. **Make evaluation effortless.** The press kit, key facts and contact details must be easy to find for someone short on time who is comparing studios.
3. **Only what is real and public.** Present only confirmed facts about the released game, and keep unannounced projects completely off the site.
4. **Built to grow.** One game is ready and more are coming soon, so every structure assumes a catalog of several titles.
