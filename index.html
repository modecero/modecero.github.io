Mode Cero is an independent studio making original casual games. Our name comes from the Amstrad CPC’s Mode 0 (“cero” is Spanish for zero).

  1. 01 Whirlpop

    Whirlpop is a 100% original HTML5 puzzler that brings together two of the most addictive genres ever made, falling blocks and match-3, by putting them on a spinning board. It runs instantly on any device, mobile or desktop, and is built for the endless “one more go.”

    Genre
    Puzzle: falling blocks meets match-3
    Platform
    HTML5, mobile and desktop browsers
